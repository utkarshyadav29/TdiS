Our Capabilities

International Standards

We facilitate comprehensive certification processes across a wide range of global directives and standards. Our engineers possess the expertise to evaluate, document, and submit your industrial equipment for international compliance.

ATEX Directive

Certification for equipment intended for use in potentially explosive atmospheres (2014/34/EU). We handle ignition hazard assessments and technical files.

PED Certification

Pressure Equipment Directive (2014/68/EU) compliance for design, manufacture, and conformity assessment of pressure equipment.

UKCA Marking

The UK Conformity Assessed marking for goods placed on the market in Great Britain, replacing the CE mark post-Brexit.

CE Marking

Conformité Européenne marking indicating health, safety, and environmental protection standards for products sold within the EEA.

PESO (India)

Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization certification for products operating in hazardous environments within India.

SIL Certification

Safety Integrity Level (IEC 61508) assessment to quantify the risk reduction provided by a safety function.

Machinery Directive

Compliance with 2006/42/EC ensuring the safety of machinery and related equipment in the European market.

Deliverables

Technical Documentation

A critical component of certification is the Technical Construction File (TCF). We prepare comprehensive, audit-ready documentation.

01 Risk Assessment & Hazard Analysis

Detailed identification of potential hazards in accordance with EN ISO 12100 or specific directive requirements, outlining mitigation strategies and residual risks.

02 Essential Health & Safety Requirements

A comprehensive checklist mapping the product's design and protective measures against the mandatory EHSRs of the applicable directives.

03 Operation & Maintenance Manuals

Professionally drafted manuals including safety instructions, installation guides, operating procedures, and maintenance schedules complying with standard requirements.

04 Declaration of Conformity (DoC)

Preparation of the formal legal document stating that the product complies with all relevant directives and standards, ready for manufacturer signature.
